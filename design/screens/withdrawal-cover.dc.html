What k does not tell you k counts withdrawals, not people. One person withdrawing the same amount ten times counts ten times — so k is an upper bound on the size of your crowd, never a measurement of it. Read it as at most this many, and possibly far fewer. It is also per publicAssetId. One token registered under several ids does not pool its cover — withdrawals under different ids share no anonymity set.
