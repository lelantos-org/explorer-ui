~ One warning survives, and it sets three rules

Deposit and withdraw separate by ΔE 7.5 under deuteranopia — inside the 6–8 band, which is legal only with a second channel of encoding. So, not optional anywhere in this explorer:

01A legend is always present, and each series is directly labelled where it can be.
02A 2px surface gap between adjacent fills, so bars separate by geometry as well as hue.
03Inflow is solid, outflow is dashed. The flow chart reads correctly in greyscale.
Colour carries meaning, once

A kind hue means the same thing in the bars, the legend and the row badges. Status colours are reserved — unique, thin, covered — and never reused as a fifth series. Each ships with a glyph and a word, so cover is never colour alone.

Refused

No dual axis — two measures at different scales get two charts. No rainbow ramp for magnitude. No value printed on every point. Grid and axes stay recessive at 1px dashed. Text wears text colours; a swatch beside it carries identity.
